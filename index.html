  • Week. Friday to Thursday, aligned to the Friday read. Part A covers the closed week, Fri 4 Sep to Thu 10 Sep. Part B covers the week ahead, Fri 11 Sep to Thu 17 Sep, which includes today.
  • Milestones. The four dated stages in this sheet — Product complete date (PRD), Design complete date, Dev complete date, QA complete Date. There is no launch-date or kickoff column, so QA complete stands in for the launch check and “moved on” is inferred from Status: a dev date counts as moved on when Status is In QA or Done, a QA date when Status is Done.
  • Planned or actual — the key caveat. Each stage cell holds either a plan or a completion, unlabelled. A date in the past means only that the date arrived; the row's owner is the only one who can say whether it was met.
  • Overdue. A stage date has passed while Status is still at or before that stage (the earliest such date is shown), or the Est. Launch month has ended without Done. Lateness is counted to 11 Sep. Broken sequence: QA before dev, dev before design, or design before PRD.
  • Holder. In dev → Dev Owner. In QA → QA Owner. In design → Design Owner. In product work / Ready to be picked up / Planned → Product Owner. No name in that column → TBD.
  • Risk flags on Part B rows: due today; dev not started with a dev date this week; QA due while still in dev; no dev or QA named; QA date before dev date; dev date within two days of design date; a recorded blocker; one person carrying three or more milestones in the window; the item's Est. Launch is this month.
  • Blocked, on hold, waiting. Any open row whose Status or Reason of Delay says blocked, or that carries any Reason of Delay. The label is read from the reason text.
  • Missing dates. Percentages are of 41 scoped rows, except design dates, which are of the 22 rows with Design Required = Yes. “Fully dated” means PRD, dev and QA dates present, plus design where required.
  • Date parsing. Day-first prose (9th Sep,2026), month-first prose (September 15, 2026) and US MM/DD/YYYY are all read. Dates with no year are assumed to be 2026. A bare month (Jan,2026) and prose (TBD Conversation team) count as no date and never as a passed milestone.
